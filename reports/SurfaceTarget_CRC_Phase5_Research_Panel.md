# SurfaceTarget-CRC
## Phase 5 — Research Panel and Validation Roadmap

This report summarizes 15 exploratory research candidates selected from 948 colorectal tumor-associated transcriptomic candidates.

The panel combines cross-scenario ranking agreement with complementary mechanistic research hypotheses.

It does not represent a validated therapeutic ranking, safety assessment, or clinical recommendation.

## Research evidence

- TCGA: tumor-associated RNA evidence, including computationally low-MSI discovery cases.
- CPTAC: paired MSS colon tumor/normal proteomics, subject to protein-specific coverage.
- UniProt/HPA: topology and descriptive normal-tissue expression annotations.
- DepMap: colorectal adenocarcinoma CRISPR fitness measurements without confirmed MSS status in this analysis.

## Ranking limitations

The protein-first and normal-review-first rankings are highly correlated.
Only CA9, ADAM12, and NPC1L1 appeared in the top 20 under all three scenarios.
Cross-scenario agreement does not constitute independent biological validation.

## Scientific limitations

Bulk tumor RNA and proteomics do not establish malignant-cell-specific expression.

UniProt topology does not directly demonstrate antibody accessibility or internalization.

HPA staining does not establish clinical safety or predict therapeutic toxicity.

CRISPR knockout effects do not directly measure antibody-drug conjugate efficacy.

Missing evidence has not been interpreted as negative biological evidence.

All 948 original research candidates remain preserved in the complete dataset.

No targets have been experimentally validated by this project.
